Md Mahamudul Hasan Pranto

Mechanical Engineering Graduate & Researcher

Md Mahamudul Hasan Pranto earned his B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. His research focuses on renewable energy systems, advanced thermal management, and the water–energy nexus. His interests also include green hydrogen production and storage optimization through techno-economic assessment. His broader work covers hybrid nano-enhanced phase change materials, photovoltaic thermal (PVT)-integrated solar desalination, and capacitive deionization. He aims to advance UN Sustainable Development Goals 6 (Clean Water), 7 (Affordable Energy), and 13 (Climate Action).
