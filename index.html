Md Mahamudul Hasan Pranto

Lecturer, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering (BAUST Khulna)

Md Mahamudul Hasan Pranto earned his B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. His research focuses on nano-material-enhanced phase change materials (PCMs) and their applications in renewable energy systems, including PVT systems, solar stills, battery thermal management, and controlled environment agriculture. He also has growing research interests in advanced materials for electrochemical water splitting and renewable hydrogen production. He aims to advance UN Sustainable Development Goals 6 (Clean Water), 7 (Affordable Energy), and 13 (Climate Action).

PV/T Integrated Solar Desalination Using Hybrid Nano-PCM

UGC Bangladesh Funded (FY 2024–2025)

Synthesized 6 hybrid nano-enhanced PCM composites (+1062% thermal conductivity, 2.44 W/m·K) characterized via DTC-25, DSC, TGA, FTIR, and FLIR. Fabricated an Arduino-instrumented 3-panel PV/T collector with a 6-slope hexagonal solar still achieving 19.78% peak electrical efficiency, 74.94% thermal efficiency, 3.20 L/m²/day yield, and 95.88% water cost reduction ($0.0105/L).
